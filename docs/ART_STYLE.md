# Art Direction

AI NOTE: Current visuals are original raster drawings, not copied franchise assets or proof of real work.

- Aerial 2D room plan, slight furniture depth, hard pixel edges, y-sorted personnel, integer tile sizes, and `imageSmoothingEnabled=false`.
- Original room geometry, desks/terminals, shelves, kitchen cups, and original 12x16 sprites are generated directly in the browser. No atlas-generation step is required.
- Industrial charcoal and forest structure with signal-green data, amber setup warnings, coral safety state, and distinct team uniform colors. Compact operational typography, not a marketing layout.
- Local Barlow Condensed headings and IBM Plex Mono text via pinned OFL Fontsource packages. Lucide icons name tools, with accessible labels/tooltips for icon-only controls.
- Roster, map, and status must agree with server state. Do not animate pretend research, manufacture sales, or make idle bodies look as though paid work is happening.
- Keep layout/text usable on desktop/mobile and respect reduced motion. Validate actual Canvas pixels and local assets, not only an accessibility snapshot.
- No franchise logos or copied game sprites. This UI's vault theme must not appear in sold products. Future richer art, directional walks, sound, and mockup templates remain separate work.
