# Safety And Compliance

AI NOTE: These are launch requirements, not a claim that placeholder inspectors or disconnected integrations already enforce them.

- Owner-approved capabilities and budgets, restricted provider sessions, and server-validated tools are mandatory. Prompts, model votes, and external text cannot create authority.
- Draft-first publication with one-use approval tied to the exact inspected payload/version/hash. No environment-variable autopublish bypass.
- Original products and truthful previews only. No competitor images, copied listing text, trademarks, celebrity/franchise characters, misleading claims, or vault-theme IP in products. Automated IP filters are not legal clearance.
- Verify current Etsy creativity, seller eligibility, disclosure, API, retention, and rate-limit rules before live work. Inaccessible policy documentation is an unresolved launch gate.
- External text is untrusted data, not executable instructions. Deny arbitrary paths, raw shell/network tools, ambient MCP/plugins, unknown permissions, and nested untracked agents.
- Actual subscriptions count as cash once; included AI allowance is separate usage. Zero effective limits until commissioned; no top-ups or paid fallback. Retain unknown spend/write liabilities and never blindly replay dispatched operations.
- Trial Stop and campaign Emergency Suspend remain authoritative, including across restart. Abort/disconnect must be verified with real adapters; a local timeout cannot undo a remote side effect.
- Only genuine reconciled campaign cash counts toward the goal; no fixture/forecast/pre-existing-shop revenue, tax collections, or provisional payouts.
- Credentials stay in private gitignored storage and a trusted broker, never model context, child environments, public files, URLs, logs, or screenshots. Loopback bind, origin/session-token checks, and CSP are implemented; do not expose this single-user service publicly.
- No private chain-of-thought collection/display. Store concise decisions, provenance, approved artifacts, actions, and actual outcomes.
